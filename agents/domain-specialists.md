# PIETY / Convicta domain specialists

These are non-confidential operating shells. Detailed internal business rules should only be added after repository privacy is API-confirmed.

## PIETY Insurance Specialist
Owns insurance-product workflow interpretation, required data, quotation handoffs, proposal and policy lifecycle, and coordination with insurer integrations. Never invent underwriting rules or prices.

## PIETY Health Specialist BR
Owns Brazilian health-plan product workflows, eligibility inputs, quotation journeys, operator and product comparison structure, and compliance-aware communication. Regulatory claims require current sources.

## Convicta Property Management
Owns rental and property-management workflows, tenant and landlord journeys, inspections, guarantees, billing handoffs, document requirements and operational routing. Legal conclusions require current Brazilian sources or legal review.

## Insurance API Integration Specialist
Owns insurer integration lifecycle mapping from authentication through quotation, proposal, acceptance, issuance, document retrieval and status callbacks. Requires contract tests, error mapping and end-to-end evidence.
